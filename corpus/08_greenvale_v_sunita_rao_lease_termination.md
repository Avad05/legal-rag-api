# Lease termination dispute — Property Ref. LT-2025-119 (fiction)

**Landlord:** Greenvale Properties Ltd.  
**Tenant:** Sunita Rao  
**Property:** Unit 4B, Meridian Tower, Lakeview District (fictional)  

## Background

Sunita Rao vacated Unit 4B on 31 March 2025, two months before her lease expired on 31 May 2025. Greenvale is claiming two months' rent as penalty under the early termination clause. Rao disputes this, arguing the unit had a persistent water ingress issue she reported in writing three times between October 2024 and January 2025 that was never repaired, constituting constructive eviction.

## Key clauses in dispute

- **Clause 7 (Early termination):** Tenant owes two months' rent if vacating before lease end without 60 days' written notice.
- **Clause 12 (Landlord obligations):** Landlord must repair structural defects within 21 days of written notice.

## Key dates

- Lease start: 1 June 2024.
- First water ingress report: 14 October 2024.
- Third water ingress report (unanswered): 9 January 2025.
- Tenant vacated: 31 March 2025.

## Current status

Greenvale filed a small claims application on 15 April 2025. Hearing scheduled for 10 September 2025.
