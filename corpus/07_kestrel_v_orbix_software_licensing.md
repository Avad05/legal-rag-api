# Software licensing dispute — Matter No. SL-2024-0441 (fiction)

**Parties:** Kestrel Dynamics Pvt. Ltd. v. Orbix Software Solutions  
**Court:** Commercial Division, Harlow Circuit (fictional)  
**Stage:** Pre-trial motions  

## Summary

Kestrel claims Orbix delivered a defective enterprise resource planning suite that failed acceptance testing three times between April and September 2024. Orbix counters that Kestrel's IT team misconfigured the deployment environment, voiding the warranty clause under Section 9.2 of the license agreement.

## Key clauses in dispute

- **Section 9.2 (Warranty void):** Warranty is void if client modifies default system configuration without written approval from Orbix.
- **Section 14.1 (Liability cap):** Orbix's total liability shall not exceed the fees paid in the twelve months prior to the claim.

## Key dates

- License agreement signed: 3 February 2024.
- First failed acceptance test: 22 April 2024.
- Kestrel's written notice of breach: 7 October 2024.
- Limitation period: four years under the fictional Harlow Commercial Code.

3 September 2025 — motion to dismiss Orbix's warranty void argument.
