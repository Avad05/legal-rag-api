# Founder dispute — Shareholder resolution SR-2025-07 (fiction)

**Company:** Talomir Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (fictional)  
**Parties:** Rajan Iyer (Founder, 52% shareholding) v. Meera Chandrasekar (Co-founder, 30% shareholding)  
**Matter:** Forced buyout claim  

## Background

Meera Chandrasekar filed for a forced buyout of her 30% stake after Rajan Iyer unilaterally signed a revenue-sharing agreement with a third party (Deltavox Pte. Ltd.) without board approval, allegedly in violation of Article 18 of Talomir's shareholder agreement which requires unanimous board consent for contracts exceeding ₹50 lakhs.

## Key facts

- Revenue-sharing agreement value: ₹1.2 crore over 24 months.
- Board quorum requirement under Article 6: three of four directors.
- Directors present at signing: Rajan Iyer only.
- Meera's last valuation of her stake (independent auditor): ₹85 lakhs.

## Relief sought

Chandrasekar seeks buyout at audited valuation plus 18% interest from date of breach, or alternatively, nullification of the Deltavox agreement.

## Next step

Arbitration under the fictional Talomir Shareholder Agreement Clause 22. First session: 20 October 2025.
